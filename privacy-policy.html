Privacy Policy

Focus Blocker

Last updated: 2026-07-13

1. Introduction

Focus Blocker is designed with privacy as a core principle. Most app functionality operates entirely on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what limited data may be collected and how it is used.

2. Data Collection

Focus Blocker does not require user accounts and does not collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, contacts, or messages.

All user-defined data such as:

  • Focus schedules
  • Blocked app lists
  • Session settings

is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted externally.

Focus Blocker uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google LLC, to collect anonymous usage statistics.

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect:

  • App interaction events (such as feature usage)
  • Device identifiers
  • General device and usage information

This data is used solely to:

  • Understand how users interact with the app
  • Improve reliability and performance
  • Fix bugs and enhance usability

No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

3. Usage Access Permission

Focus Blocker uses the Android Usage Access permission (Usage Stats) solely to determine which application is currently active during an active focus session.

This permission is required exclusively to enforce app blocking functionality.

Focus Blocker:

  • does not store detailed usage history
  • does not build user profiles
  • does not transmit usage data externally
  • does not access usage data outside blocking logic

All usage information processing happens locally on the device during active blocking sessions only.

4. Display Over Other Apps (Overlay Permission)

Focus Blocker uses the “Display over other apps” permission to show a blocking screen above restricted applications during an active focus session.

The app does not capture screen content, record user interactions, or collect screen data.

5. Exact Alarms and Foreground Services

Focus Blocker may use Android Exact Alarms and Foreground Services to reliably start and maintain user-created focus sessions at the times configured by the user.

These features are used exclusively to provide the core functionality of scheduled app blocking, background monitoring, and automatic recovery while a focus session is active.

Focus Blocker does not use Exact Alarms or Foreground Services for:

  • advertising
  • user tracking
  • profiling
  • collecting personal information

6. Data Sharing

Focus Blocker does not sell personal data and does not share personal information with third parties.

Anonymous usage data may be processed by trusted service providers such as Google Firebase Analytics for the purpose of improving application performance.

7. Data Security

Data transmitted through Firebase Analytics is encrypted using secure HTTPS connections.

8. Support Communication

If you voluntarily contact support (for example via email) or send a diagnostic report from within the app, you may choose to provide diagnostic information to help resolve your issue.

Such information may include:

  • App version
  • Android version
  • Device model
  • Battery optimization status
  • Exact alarm permission status
  • Premium or Free status
  • Selected blocking mode
  • Current focus session state
  • Schedule status and remaining time
  • Blocked application names and package names
  • Recent blocking decisions
  • Recent blocking history
  • Foreground service status
  • Background monitoring status
  • Service heartbeat and recovery status
  • Recent service events
  • Diagnostic logs used to verify app functionality and troubleshoot blocking behavior

This information is not collected automatically. It is received only if voluntarily provided and used exclusively for troubleshooting, verifying app functionality, investigating blocking behavior, and improving reliability.

Support reports do not include personal messages, contacts, photos, files, browsing history, or screen content.

9. Children's Privacy

Focus Blocker does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
